Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantities (MOQs)?

Minimums depend on the packaging format because setup, tooling, and production runs vary by product type.

Typical starting points on this form are: folding cartons 1,000 units, corrugated boxes 500, rigid boxes 500, floor displays 100, labels and stickers 2,000, tape 500, and most pouches or tissue formats 10,000.

If your project falls outside the standard list, submit the RFQ anyway and the PackOasis team can confirm the best achievable quantity for your structure.

What is the turnaround time on my order?

Most projects fall into an estimated 10 to 30 business day production window, depending on packaging type, order size, finish complexity, and seasonal factory capacity.

As a general guide, folding cartons and inserts are often faster, while rigid boxes and large display programs usually take longer.

For the most accurate schedule, send your specs in the RFQ form and we will quote based on your exact configuration.

Where do you ship to?

We support both North American and international shipping.

Depending on the production location and delivery destination, local taxes, import duties, or customs checks may apply and can affect final delivery timing.

If you need a destination confirmed before ordering, include it in the RFQ and we will validate the shipping path during quoting.

Do you have volume discounts or price breaks?

Yes. Higher quantities usually reduce cost per unit because setup and production costs are spread across more pieces.

If you are comparing quantity tiers, include your preferred range in the request and we can outline the most efficient pricing break for your project.

Do you offer rush orders?

Rush production may be possible, but it depends on current factory capacity, seasonality, and the packaging format you need.

The fastest way to check availability is to submit the RFQ with your deadline so we can confirm whether an accelerated schedule is realistic.

How do I place a reorder?

The simplest route is to reference your previous project with your assigned specialist or submit a new RFQ and note that it is a reorder.

If you already know the specs or prior order details, include them in the message so we can match the existing packaging program faster.

I have more questions

Use the RFQ form on this page or email hello@packoasis.com with your product type, quantity target, delivery market, and timeline.

That gives us enough context to route you to the right packaging specialist quickly.
