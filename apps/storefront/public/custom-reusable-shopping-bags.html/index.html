Non Woven Fabric

Consists of recyclable fibre layers. Its high durability and water absorption property make it popular for applications in reusable grocery bags and retail bags.

Woven Fabric

Consists of weaved yarns that are sturdy and provide high durability. It compacts easily but can maintain its original shape.

Jute Fabric

A biodegradable and compostable plant fabric made from renewable resources. Its unique texture and durability make it popular for applications in retail packaging.

Cotton Fabric

A recyclable natural plant fabric made from renewable recourses. Its flexibility and high durability make it ideal for grocery and retail product packaging.

Canvas

A plain-weave fabric made out of cotton or linen that provides durability and resistance to water.

Digital Print

Digital printing method that requires no printing plate. Designs are transferred electronically, making it cost-effective for small runs.

Silk Screen Print

Stenciling printing method using stacked layers of color. Commonly used for one to two color prints.

Water-based Ink

100% eco-friendly ink comprised of water and pigment that provides high-quality color output. Not suitable for food packaging.

Soy / Vegetable Ink

100% eco-friendly ink comprised of soy and vegetable oil and pigment that provides high-quality color output. Suitable for food packaging.

Oil Base Ink

Non-eco-friendly ink comprised of oil and pigment that provides high-quality color output.

Pantone

Pure color based on specific ink formulas to consistenly and precisely print color.

Pantone Metallic

Metallic color based on specific ink formulas to consistenly and precisely print color.

Frequently Asked Questions

Below you'll find answers to common questions around reusable bag; however, if you need additional help, don't hesitate to contact us to see how we can help!

What fabrics do you offer for reusable bags?

We offer a wide range of reusable bag material options and have the capability to source any options you need even if not be listed on our website.
Our most popular reusable bag materials include:

  • Cotton
  • Woven
  • Non-Woven
  • Jute
  • Canvas

How do you complete quality checks for reusable bags?

Our reusable bags go through stringent quality control to ensure strength, durability and versatility. The quality control tests conducted for reusable bags include:

  • Ball pressure test
  • Bond strength test
  • Tensile test
  • Compressive strength test

Are reusable bags washing machine friendly?

Yes, however it depends on your material choice. Popular reusable bag materials that are washing machine friendly include:

  • Woven
  • Cotton
  • Canvas

What is the minimum order quantity for reusable bags?

The minimum order quantity for custom reusable bags is 500-100 units in order to optimize your budget for production costs.
However this may vary depending on the type of reusable you choose.

What are my printing options for reusable bags?

Yes, you certainly can!
Popular printing methods for reusable bags include:

  • Screen printing
  • Heat transfer
  • Gravure
